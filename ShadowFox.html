Get to know Dr. Anupriya

Your trusted partner in family dentistry

Bringing over 20 years of clinical expertise, Dr. Anupriya stands as a trusted name in modern dentistry in Hosur. After graduating in 2000, she dedicated six impactful years to Mathura Clinic before founding Sakthi Dental Clinic in 2004.

Her work spans beyond clinic boundaries, serving for over a decade as an esteemed consultant with the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital. Together with a seasoned panel of specialists, she delivers pain-free dental care backed by the latest dental innovations.

Our Mission

To redefine family oral healthcare by delivering gentle, compassionate, and advanced treatments in a soothing setting, combining state-of-the-art tech with warm empathy.

Our Vision

To set new standards in community oral care, ensuring world-class dental solutions and preventive education are welcoming and accessible to all.
